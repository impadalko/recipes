## Ingredients

* 360 g warm water
* 15 g sugar
* 5 g active dry yeast
* 540 g flour
* 2 tsp salt

## For Boiling and Assembly

* 3 L water
* 3 tbsp g sugar
* 1 tsp baking soda
* Sesame seeds

## Instructions

1. In a large mixing bowl, combine the water, sugar or malt syrup, yeast, flour, and salt. Stir by hand just until a shaggy dough forms and no dry flour remains (let the dough rest for 15 minutes to make this easier).
2. Cover the bowl tightly and place it in the refrigerator overnight (12 to 24 hours) for a slow cold fermentation.
3. Line two large baking sheets with parchment paper and lightly grease the paper.
4. Turn the cold dough out onto a clean work surface. Divide the dough into 8 equal pieces (or 12 pieces for smaller bagels). 
5. Shape each piece into a tight ball by cupping your hand over the dough and moving it in a circular pattern on the counter.
6. Gently flatten each ball. Lightly oil your hands, press your finger straight through the center of the dough to create a hole, and stretch it into a ring about 2 inches wide.
7. Place the shaped bagels onto the greased parchment paper, spacing them a few inches apart. Cover loosely and let rest for 20 minutes.
8. Preheat your oven to 220°C.
9. In a large, wide pot, bring the 2.8 L of water to a boil, then reduce the heat to medium-low to maintain a gentle simmer. Stir in the sugar or malt syrup and the baking soda.
10. Drop 2 or 3 bagels at a time into the simmering water. Cook for 1 minute, then flip them over using a slotted spoon or spider and cook for 1 more minute. 
11. Remove the bagels, let the excess water drain off, and place them back onto the baking sheets. Immediately dip them with your desired toppings.
12. Bake for 15 minutes, then flip the bagels over and bake for another 5 to 10 minutes until deep golden brown.
