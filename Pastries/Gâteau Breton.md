## Ingredients

* 500 g plain flour
* 300 g sugar
* 250 g unsalted butter, room temperature
* ½ tsp salt
* 2 large eggs
* 2 egg yolks (plus 1 additional egg yolk for the egg wash)
* 1 tsp vanilla extract
* 1 tbsp rum
* 3 tbsp jam
* Sliced almonds (optional, for garnish)
* Raw sugar (for sprinkling)

## Instructions

1. In a large bowl, mix the flour, sugar, and salt. 
2. Rub the room-temperature butter into the dry ingredients with your hands until the texture is sandy.
3. Add the 2 eggs, 2 egg yolks, vanilla, and rum. Mix until a dough forms.
4. Split the dough into two equal parts.
5. Press half of the dough into a springform pan. Spread the jam evenly over the base, leaving a small border around the edges.
6. Place the other half of the dough on top of the jam layer. Even it out with a spatula.
7. Brush the top with the reserved egg yolk, use a fork to mark a crosshatch pattern, and sprinkle with raw sugar and sliced almonds (if using).
8. Bake in a preheated oven at 200°C for 20 minutes. 
9. Lower the temperature to 180°C and bake for another 20 minutes until golden brown.
10. Cool completely before serving.
