## Crust Ingredients

* 300 g all-purpose flour
* 120 g sugar
* a pinch of salt
* 1 tsp baking powder
* Lemon zest (from ½ lemon)
* 140 g unsalted butter, cold and cubed
* 1 large egg

## Filling Ingredients

* 150 g cream cheese
* 250 g goiabada cremosa

## Instructions

1. In the bowl of the stand mixer with the paddle attachment, mix the flour, sugar, salt, baking powder, lemon zest, and cold cubed butter. Pulse until the mixture reaches a coarse, sandy texture.
2. Add the egg and mix briefly just until incorporated. The mixture should remain crumbly with irregular lumps.
3. Spread half of the crumbly dough mixture over the bottom of the pie mold and press it down lightly to form a loose base.
4. Dollop half of the cream cheese over the dough base.
5. Gently spread the goiabada over the cream cheese layer.
6. Dollop the remaining cream cheese over the guava layer.
7. Cover the filling with the remaining half of the crumbly dough mixture. Leave this top layer loose and unpressed.
8. Bake in a preheated oven at 170°C (340°F) for about 35 minutes until golden on top.
9. Let the pastry cool completely (about 2 hours) before removing from the mold and slicing.
