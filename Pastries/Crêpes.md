## Ingredients

* 250 g flour
* 4 eggs
* 450 ml slightly warm milk
* 2 tbsp dark rum
* 1 tsp vanilla extract
* 25 g sugar
* 1 pinch salt
* 50 g melted butter

## Instructions

1. Melt the butter in the microwave and gently warm the milk until it is barely lukewarm.
2. In a large bowl, mix the sifted flour, sugar, and salt.
3. Add the eggs and melted butter to the dry ingredients, then gradually whisk in the lukewarm milk, whisking continuously to prevent lumps from forming.
4. Stir in the rum along with the vanilla. 
5. Let the batter rest for at least 30 minutes before cooking.
6. Heat a small pat of butter in a 20 cm crêpe pan or skillet.
7. Cook the crêpes on both sides until golden brown. Repeat with the remaining batter.
8. As you finish each crêpe, stack it on a plate and cover it loosely with a sheet of aluminum foil to keep them warm and soft, preventing the edges from drying out. Serve warm.
