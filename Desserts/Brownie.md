## Ingredients

* 200 g unsalted butter
* 400 g granulated sugar
* 75 g unsweetened cocoa powder
* 3 large eggs, room temperature
* 1 tbsp coffee.
* 1 tbsp vanilla extract
* 1 tsp salt
* 120 g all-purpose flour
* 270 g semisweet chocolate chips
* 100 g walnuts or hazelnuts, coarsely chopped (optional)

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 180°C.
2. In a large microwave-safe bowl, melt the butter in the microwave in 20-second intervals, stirring between each interval until fully melted.
3. Add the sugar and cocoa powder to the melted butter and whisk vigorously for 30 seconds.
4. Whisk in the eggs, coffee, vanilla extract, and salt until fully incorporated.
5. Add the flour, chocolate chips, and chopped nuts. Mix together with a spatula just until combined. 
6. Spread the thick batter evenly into the  pan.
7. Bake for 35 to 40 minutes for super fudgy brownies.
8. Let the brownies cool completely in the pan before slicing.
