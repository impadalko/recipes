## Ingredients

* 395 g sweetened condensed milk
* 30 g chocolate powder
* 200 g cream
## Instructions

1. In a saucepan, combine the sweetened condensed milk, chocolate powder, and cream.
2. Cook over medium-low heat, stirring continuously. 
3. Remove from the heat once the mixture reaches a syrup consistency. It should be slightly thickened but still fluid and pourable.
