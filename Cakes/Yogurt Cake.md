## Ingredients

* 5 large eggs 
* 170 g plain yogurt
* 170 ml neutral oil (same measure as the yogurt container)
* 300 g sugar 
* 240 g  flour 
* 1 tbsp baking powder
* 1 tsp vanilla extract
* ½ tsp salt
## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 180°C and thoroughly grease a bundt baking pan. 
2. In a blender, add the eggs, oil, yogurt, sugar, and vanilla extract. Blend very well until the mixture is smooth. 
3. In a separate large bowl, add the all-purpose flour, baking powder and salt. Pour the blended liquid mixture over the flour and whisk until combined.
4. Pour the batter into the greased pan and bake for 45 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean. Let the cake cool before removing it from the pan.
