## Ingredients

* 3 large baking apples
* 2 tsp apple cider vinegar
* 120 g all-purpose flour
* 1 tsp baking powder
* 1 tsp salt
* 100 g unsalted butter, at room temperature
* 100 g sugar
* 25 g light brown sugar
* 2 large eggs
* 3 tbsp dark rum
* ½ tsp vanilla extract
* 35 g sugar (for the top)
* 1 tbsp powdered sugar (optional, for dusting)

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 180°C.
2. Core the apples. Cut them into 0,5 cm to 1 cm cubes. Keep them small so they become tender during baking. 
3. Place the diced apples in a large bowl and toss with the apple cider vinegar.
4. In a small bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder, and salt.
5. In a separate bowl, beat the butter, white sugar, and brown sugar with an electric mixer until light and fluffy.
6. Beat in the first egg on high speed until fully incorporated and smooth. Add the second egg and repeat.
7. Mix in the rum, vanilla extract, and half of the flour mixture. Once the flour is incorporated, add the rest of the flour and gently fold it in with a spatula.
8. Fold the apples into the batter until evenly distributed. 
9. Transfer the batter to the pan, pressing it in firmly. Smooth the top and sprinkle evenly with white sugar.
10. Bake in the preheated oven for about 45 minutes, or until a skewer inserted into the center comes out clean.
11. Let the cake cool in the pan for about 30 minutes. 
12. Invert the cooled cake onto a wire rack, place a serving plate on the bottom, and flip it back right-side up. Dust the top with powdered sugar before serving.
