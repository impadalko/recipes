Original recipe: https://www.loveandlemons.com/pickled-red-onions/
## Ingredients

* 1 small red onion
* 240 ml white vinegar
* 240 ml water
* 30 g sugar
* 1 tbsp salt
* Aromatics
	* 1 cinnamon stick
	* 1 bay leaf
	* Some peppercorns
	* Some cloves

## Instructions

1. Thinly slice the onions and add it to a glass jar. Add the aromatics to the jar.
2. In a medium saucepan, heat the vinegar, water, sugar, and salt over medium heat. Stir until the sugar and salt dissolve, about 1 minute.
3. Let the liquid cool, then pour it over the onions until they are completely submerged.
4. Set the jars aside to cool to room temperature, then cover and store them in the refrigerator.
